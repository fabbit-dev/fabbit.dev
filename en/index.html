Fabbit. Pocket multitool for electronics.

We’re developing a pocket oscilloscope, logic analyzer and retro console. A project by high school students.

01Functions

Instruments
on one board.

We’re developing the functions below. CH32H417 handles the bench supply and multimeter.

    01 /

    Interface demo. On-screen values are illustrative. Doom illustrates the retro concept; NES is confirmed on the bench setup.

    Early ideas: neural accelerator, cable tester, bus analyzer, programmer and terminal. These are outside the current target set.

    02Teardown

    We take it apart
    layer by layer.

    Screen and buttons up front. The FPGA, ADC and battery sit under the back cover.

    03Blueprint

    88×150×22 mm

    The size of the original Game Boy, one centimeter thinner. The case model lives in the repo: download fabbit_case.FCStd and print your own.
